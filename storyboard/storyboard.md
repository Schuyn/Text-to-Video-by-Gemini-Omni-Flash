# Dream Journey to the West — July 5 Returning Player Campaign Storyboard

## Project Information

- **Theme:** Dream Journey to the West — July 5 Returning Player Campaign Trailer
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Maximum duration per generated shot:** 10 seconds
- **Tone and Style:** Heroic, playful, high-energy Chinese fantasy MMORPG cinematic CG
- **Core Message:** The returning player campaign begins on July 5. The rewards are fully loaded—come back and join the fight.

## Shot 5–8 Timing and Keyframe Handoffs

- Shots 1–4 retain their existing 0–30s timing. Shots 5–8 use 8 + 10 + 7 + 5 = 30 seconds, keeping the complete film at 60 seconds and every shot at or below 10 seconds. All motion-prompt timings are local to their own shot.
- The reward list is split across Shots 5 and 6 without dropping any offer: returning-player gift pack, experience boosts, exclusive badge, Summer Spirit exchanges for limited rewards, and login rewards of Rainbow Fruit and gems. Long Taizi's wording is shortened to fit a clear delivery before Jian Xiake's response.
- Shot 5 end → Shot 6 start: exactly the first three reward objects remain visible; Long Taizi is preparing his reveal and Jian Xiake's sword is fully sheathed. Regenerate the existing `key_frames/shot_5/end.png`, which still shows all six rewards, using the revised `prompts/shot_5/end_frame.md`; use the replacement image as the Shot 6 start reference.
- Shot 6 end → Shot 7 start: all six rewards are gone, Jian Xiake holds his drawn sword, and the trio faces forward in the same battle formation. Shot 7 end → Shot 8 start uses the golden energy sweep for the cut into the graphic card.
