# Dream Journey to the West — July 5 Returning Player Campaign Storyboard

## Project Information

- **Theme:** Dream Journey to the West — July 5 Returning Player Campaign Trailer
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Tone and Style:** Heroic, playful, high-energy Chinese fantasy MMORPG cinematic CG
- **Core Message:** The returning player campaign begins on July 5. The rewards are fully loaded—come back and join the fight.

## Shot List

| Shot | Timecode | Visual | Dialogue / Audio | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–5s | The Chang'an City martial arts arena at sunset. Jian Xiake stands with his back to the camera, a long sword in hand and his robes fluttering in the wind. | Wind ambience and a faint metallic sword resonance. | Open with a striking heroic image that evokes the return of a legendary warrior. |
| 2 | 5–15s | Jian Xiake turns around and looks directly into the camera with a determined gaze. | **Jian Xiake:** “July 5—do me a favor: come back and fight!” | Keep the delivery concise, direct, and roguishly confident. |
| 3 | 15–20s | Gu Jingling bounces into frame from the side, plants her hands on her hips, and tilts her head toward Jian Xiake. | **Gu Jingling:** “Well, look at you—asking for help for once.” | Lively, playful, and teasing. |
| 4 | 20–30s | Long Taizi descends from the opposite side with his spear, lands elegantly, and casually brushes off his clothes. | **Long Taizi:** “Fighting alone would be boring—I’ve already arranged everything.” | Gentle and self-assured, with a hint of pride. |
| 5 | 30–45s | The three heroes stand side by side. Gu Jingling counts the benefits on her fingers while Long Taizi gestures to reveal magical reward effects. | **Gu Jingling:** “A returning-player gift pack, experience boosts, an exclusive badge…” **Long Taizi:** “Plus Summer Spirit exchanges for limited rewards—and log in to claim Rainbow Fruit and gems!” | Deliver the lines quickly, building excitement as the reward list grows. |
| 6 | 45–52s | Jian Xiake draws his sword and points toward the distance as all three heroes assume battle-ready poses. | **Jian Xiake:** “Then what are we waiting for?” | Ignite the heroic energy; let the three exchange a confident look. |
| 7 | 52–57s | All three leap forward and charge toward the camera, or transition into a rapid montage of in-game CG combat. | **All three:** “July 5—Dream Journey to the West is calling you back!” | End the character sequence on an explosive, triumphant beat. |
| 8 | 57–60s | Display the campaign title, date, key reward phrases, and game logo. | The music peaks, then resolves decisively. | Final campaign information card. |
