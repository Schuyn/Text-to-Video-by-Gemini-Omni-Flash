# Shot 1 — Motion Prompt

**Action:**

Create one continuous five-second shot that transitions smoothly from the supplied start frame to the supplied end frame. As anticipation builds, he subtly shifts into readiness and slowly raises the sword in his right hand from a low resting angle to the slightly elevated diagonal position shown in the end frame. He must not turn around, reveal his face, walk, attack, or perform any additional action. No cuts and no new subjects enter the frame.

**Character Motion:**

Keep Jian Xiake's movement restrained, deliberate, and confident. His feet remain planted in exactly the same place. His posture stays upright while his shoulders square very slightly and his right forearm lifts the sword with controlled strength. His left arm remains relaxed by his side. Add only subtle natural breathing and a small tightening of his sword grip. His high ponytail, long sleeves, robe panels, and waist ribbons respond continuously to the wind with realistic secondary motion. **Camera Motion:**

Use gentle cinematic easing at the beginning and end of the move. Do not pan, orbit, tilt, roll, zoom, shake, rack focus, or cross around the character.

Do not add crowds, characters, creatures, magical effects, text, logos, or game UI.

Keep the sound cinematic and spacious, with no dialogue, narration, crowd noise, combat impacts, or prominent music.
